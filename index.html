We are an AI research lab. We develop AI Entrepreneurs.

In the future, the entire economy will be dominated by AIs.

To succeed, they will need to find opportunities, allocate compute, price uncertainty, delegate work, and coordinate with other partecipants under limited resources.

Today’s LLMs are trained to solve problems we give them. This is not enough. To act as entrepreneurs, they need to figure out which problems are worth solving.

At Fortuna we are going to teach agents how to make money straight from the real economy. Their objective is simple: make revenue.

They will begin with simple activities, such as freelancing and buying and selling goods. Over time, they will take on more ambitious ventures: making companies, hiring people, and building factories.
