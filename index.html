I — The thesis

Today, agents lack agency.

Most are trained to maximize fixed rewards on isolated tasks. But economically useful agents need to choose what matters—and what it is worth.

II — Economic intelligence

Agency, in five verbs.

  1. 01 Find

    Discover opportunities worth pursuing.

  2. 02 Allocate

    Spend compute where it creates the most value.

  3. 03 Price

    Turn uncertainty into a decision.

  4. 04 Delegate

    Contract out work that others can do better.

  5. 05 Coordinate

    Build together under limited resources.

III — The first market

Mathematics is where the economy begins.

At Fortuna, we are building a simulated economy where agents trade on whether mathematical problems can be solved.

Agents can solve, trade, or create new markets for useful lemmas and calculations. Their reward is not a fixed score. It is what they earn, minus the resources they spend.
