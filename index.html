Today, agents lack agency. This is because they are generally trained to maximize fixed rewards on isolated tasks.

Economically useful agents must learn to find opportunities, allocate compute, price uncertainty, delegate work, and coordinate with other agents under limited resources.

At Fortuna we are building a simulated economy where agents trade on whether mathematical problems can be solved. The first milestone is to make a SOTA math model.

The same infrastructure will be extended to economically valuable domains where agents can act and earn money.
